FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Get the details you need to make a service enquiry.

Which properties do you service?

We work with residential, commercial and industrial properties. Common enquiries come from homeowners, restaurants, offices and property teams.

Can you help with a commercial fridge or cold room?

Yes. The refrigeration service covers commercial fridges, cold rooms, table chillers and water chillers. Share the equipment type and symptoms when you get in touch.

Can I book preventive maintenance?

Yes. Tell us which equipment or systems need attention and whether you have a preferred service schedule.

What should I include in my request?

Share your area, property type, equipment details, and what you are experiencing. Photos, model numbers and error codes help the team understand the issue.

How do I request a quote?

Message the team on WhatsApp with the scope and location. The team can confirm what information or inspection is needed to prepare a quote.
